Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Where we deliver

We currently deliver across India through courier and delivery partners.

Delivery charges

A delivery fee is shown at checkout. Delivery is free when your order value is above the free-delivery amount shown in your cart.

Processing and delivery time

Oils are packed fresh. Orders are usually dispatched within 1 to 3 working days. Delivery normally takes 3 to 8 working days after dispatch depending on your location. Remote locations may take longer.

Tracking

Once your order is shipped you get the courier details by email (if provided) and can check status any time on our Track Order page.

Delivery problems

Please check the parcel on delivery. If it is damaged or leaking, contact us within 48 hours (see Refund & Cancellation Policy). If you enter a wrong or incomplete address, re-delivery charges may apply.
