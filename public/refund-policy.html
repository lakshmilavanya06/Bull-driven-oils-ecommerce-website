Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Cancellation

You can cancel an order free of charge before it is packed or shipped. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 70959 24929 with your order number as soon as possible. Once an order is shipped it can no longer be cancelled.

Returns

Because edible oils are consumable products, we cannot accept returns of opened or used products. We will replace or refund your order if:

  • the product arrives damaged, leaking or tampered with,
  • you receive the wrong product or quantity, or
  • the product is clearly defective.

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos or a short video of the parcel and product.

Refunds

Approved refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) within 5 to 7 working days after approval; the exact time depends on your bank. For cash on delivery orders we refund by bank or UPI transfer. If we cancel an order, or a payment was deducted but the order failed, you will be refunded in full.
