Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

We respect your privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you order: name, mobile number, email (optional), delivery address, order notes and order details. Payment details are entered on the secure Razorpay screen and are never stored by us. Your cart is kept in your own browser only.

How we use it

To process and deliver your order, send order confirmations and delivery updates (by email or phone/WhatsApp), handle refunds and support, and keep business records as required by law.

Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to complete your order: Razorpay (payments), courier or delivery partners (name, phone, address) and our email provider. We do not sell your data.

Storage and retention

Order data is stored on our secure server and kept as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes. Abandoned checkout attempts are deleted automatically after 7 days.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The owner's admin area uses a session cookie for login only.
